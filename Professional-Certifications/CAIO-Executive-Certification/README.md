# Proposed Chief AI Officer (CAIO) Executive Certification Program

**Status:** Proposal  
**Program scope:** 200 academic hours  
**Target audience:** Executives and senior professionals with 10+ years of professional experience  
**Approach:** Vendor-agnostic  
**Written and developed by:** Yuval Sinay  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 21 August 2026

## Purpose

This proposed executive certification program prepares senior leaders to establish, govern, secure, scale, and continuously improve enterprise AI capabilities. It treats AI as an organizational transformation capability rather than a standalone technology.

**Strategy → Data → Governance → Technology & Architecture → Risk & Security → Assurance & Operations → Transformation & Workforce → Economics & Value → Board Accountability**

## Mandatory Capstone Project

Each participant must complete and defend an applied AI Business Process Transformation Project using one of two tracks:

1. **Transform an existing organizational process using AI**, documenting the AS-IS process and the AI-enabled TO-BE process.
2. **Introduce a new AI-enabled organizational process or capability** that is materially enabled or improved by AI.

The capstone must include business or mission justification, an AI suitability assessment and non-AI alternative, AS-IS and TO-BE process models, human-AI task allocation, data and governance requirements, a vendor-neutral conceptual architecture, risk and cybersecurity assessment, Responsible AI and human oversight, legal/privacy/IP considerations, TEVV and measurable acceptance criteria, implementation and change roadmap, financial/value analysis, KPIs/KRIs, monitoring, incident response, business continuity, rollback, and exit strategy.

### Final Presentation

- **Executive presentation:** 20 minutes
- **Panel defense:** 15 minutes

Candidates must demonstrate the judgment to **Approve → Modify → Delay → Reject → Terminate** an AI initiative based on evidence.

## Core References

The program is vendor-agnostic and should use current versions of authoritative references, including ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 23894, ISO/IEC 22989, ISO/IEC 23053, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, the NIST Generative AI Profile, NIST AI TEVV guidance, the EU AI Act, MITRE ATLAS, OWASP guidance for Generative AI and Agentic AI security, and relevant cybersecurity, privacy, enterprise architecture, procurement, records, and risk-management standards.

## Vendor-Agnostic Principle

The program does not require proficiency in, endorse, or provide preferential treatment to any specific cloud provider, foundation model provider, model family, AI platform, agent framework, data platform, security vendor, consulting methodology, or commercial certification.

Commercial technologies may be used only as comparative case studies.
