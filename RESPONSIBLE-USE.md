# Responsible Use and Safety Notice

## Purpose

The materials in this repository are intended solely for lawful, ethical, authorized, educational, research, defensive cybersecurity, security-engineering, risk-management, resilience, and protective purposes.

## Authorization and Laboratory Testing

Use the materials only on systems, networks, applications, accounts, devices, data, and environments that you own, that you are expressly authorized to assess or operate, or that are provided specifically for lawful cybersecurity education, simulation, or laboratory testing.

Authorization should be documented before testing begins and should cover the intended scope, methods, systems, time period, data, and potential effects of the activity.

Security testing, experiments, simulations, proof-of-concept activities, and validation exercises should be performed in an isolated and controlled laboratory environment whenever reasonably possible. Recommended safeguards include non-production systems, synthetic or lawfully obtained test data, isolated networks, restricted egress, dedicated test identities, monitoring, resource limits, rollback procedures, and appropriate human supervision.

Testing must not create an unreasonable risk to production systems, third parties, public services, safety-critical systems, or sensitive information.

## Prohibited Uses

The materials must not be used to:

1. Access, scan, test, exploit, disrupt, or interfere with systems without authorization.
2. Develop, deploy, improve, or distribute malware, ransomware, destructive code, credential theft, phishing, fraud, or malicious evasion capabilities.
3. Exfiltrate, disclose, corrupt, destroy, or manipulate data without authorization.
4. Conduct offensive cyber operations without explicit lawful authority and documented authorization.
5. Conduct unlawful surveillance, impersonation, harassment, coercion, or infringement of privacy or other rights.
6. Target critical infrastructure, healthcare, emergency services, public safety systems, or other safety-sensitive environments without appropriate authorization and safeguards.
7. Cause or materially increase the risk of physical, psychological, financial, operational, societal, or environmental harm.
8. Misrepresent modified materials as original, approved, or endorsed by the author.
