# Queensland Government FAIRA Framework

## Foundational Artificial Intelligence Risk Assessment (FAIRA)

The **Foundational Artificial Intelligence Risk Assessment (FAIRA)** framework is a Queensland Government framework for supporting transparent, accountable, and structured identification of risks when government agencies evaluate artificial intelligence solutions.

FAIRA is designed to help stakeholders identify AI-specific risks and potential mitigation actions across the AI lifecycle. It is described as "foundational" because it establishes a structured description of an AI solution, its technical components, system design, human interaction, implementation context, and associated impacts before those risks are incorporated into existing organizational risk-management processes.

## Core structure

FAIRA contains two principal assessment elements:

1. **Components analysis** - establishes a sufficiently complete description of the AI solution within its intended context of use, including technical components, system design, implementation, and human interaction.
2. **Values assessment** - identifies and manages risks arising from the AI solution in its operational context and supports their treatment through the agency's ICT risk register.

The framework is aligned with Australia's **National Framework for the Assurance of AI in Government (2024)**.

## Security and governance relevance

FAIRA can complement cybersecurity and AI governance frameworks by helping organizations establish the system and context information needed before detailed security assessment, threat modeling, assurance, control selection, or quantitative risk analysis.

Potential uses include:

- AI solution intake and governance reviews
- Pre-deployment AI risk assessment
- Identification of technical, human, implementation, and operational dependencies
- Documentation of AI-related impacts and risk owners
- Integration of AI risks into enterprise or ICT risk registers
- Identification of mitigation and assurance activities
- Supporting procurement and third-party AI assessments
- Providing context for later security testing, threat modeling, and control validation

FAIRA should be treated as a **foundational risk-identification framework**, not as a standalone cybersecurity control framework. For security engineering and adversarial-risk analysis, it can be combined with frameworks such as NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 23894, MITRE ATLAS, OWASP GenAI Security guidance, and organization-specific threat-modeling practices.

> **Terminology note:** FAIRA is the Queensland Government's *Foundational Artificial Intelligence Risk Assessment*. It is distinct from FAIR (Factor Analysis of Information Risk) and FAIR-AIR.
