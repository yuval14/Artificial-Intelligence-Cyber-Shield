# ACAAI - Accountability by Design for Agentic AI

ACAAI is an open lifecycle framework for engineering accountability into autonomous and agentic AI systems. It treats accountability as a systems-engineering property supported by coordinated organizational and technical controls across the socio-technical system.

## Framework overview

ACAAI Version 1.0 organizes accountability into six complementary domains:

1. Organizational Governance.
2. Human Oversight, Consent & Decision Authority.
3. Identity, Authority & Data Governance.
4. Observability, Explainability & Evidence.
5. Runtime Safety & Assurance.
6. Incident Response & Recovery.

The framework includes more than 100 organizational and technical controls and is intended for risk-based implementation across the agentic AI lifecycle.

## Relevance to AI Cyber Shield

ACAAI complements security-focused frameworks by emphasizing accountability, delegated authority, runtime assurance, observability, evidence, incident response, and recovery for autonomous AI systems. It is particularly useful for connecting AI governance with engineering controls for agent identity, permissions, human decision authority, runtime behavior, auditability, and operational resilience.

## Suggested mapping

| ACAAI domain | Suggested AI Cyber Shield mapping |
| --- | --- |
| Organizational Governance | AI governance, accountability, risk ownership, lifecycle policy, assurance evidence |
| Human Oversight, Consent & Decision Authority | Human approval gates, escalation, decision rights, consent, autonomy boundaries |
| Identity, Authority & Data Governance | Agent identity, least privilege, delegated authority, data access, tool permissions |
| Observability, Explainability & Evidence | Logging, traceability, explainability, audit evidence, forensic readiness |
| Runtime Safety & Assurance | Runtime controls, policy enforcement, validation, monitoring, containment, fail-safe behavior |
| Incident Response & Recovery | AI incident response, evidence preservation, rollback, recovery, lessons learned |
