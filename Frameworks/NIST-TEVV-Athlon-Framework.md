# NIST TEVV-Athlon Framework for Evaluating AI Systems

## Overview

The **TEVV-Athlon Framework for Evaluating AI Systems** is an Initial Public Draft published by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) as **NIST AI 200-2 ipd** in August 2026.

The framework provides a structured, adaptable method for designing and conducting **Test, Evaluation, Verification, and Validation (TEVV)** activities for artificial intelligence systems. It is intended to help organizations translate high-level objectives and trustworthiness requirements into measurable evaluation activities that produce evidence about AI system benefits, risks, performance, and real-world impact.

NIST designed the framework to support a broad range of AI technologies, including statistical machine learning, large language models, multimodal models, and agentic systems.

## Framework Structure

The TEVV-Athlon Framework consists of four stages:

1. **Articulate & Organize** - Define organizational goals, evaluation objectives, scope, stakeholders, and the structure of the assessment.
2. **Define & Construct** - Design the assessment events, measurement concepts, tools, metrics, data, and evaluation procedures.
3. **Apply & Measure** - Execute the assessment and collect measurement data and evidence.
4. **Synthesize & Interrogate** - Analyze results, examine limitations and trade-offs, and communicate findings to support risk and operational decisions.

The framework distinguishes between the **TEVV-Athlon Framework**, which is the methodology used to design an evaluation, and a **TEVV-Athlon**, which is the resulting assessment conducted against an AI system.

## Relationship to the NIST AI RMF

The TEVV-Athlon is designed to complement the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF):

- **Govern** and **Map** provide organizational context and objectives that inform Stage 1.
- The TEVV-Athlon operationalizes the **Measure** function by conducting structured evaluation activities.
- Results from Stage 4 can inform the **Manage** function by providing evidence for risk treatment, benefit realization, operational decisions, and control improvement.

## Why It Matters

The framework is particularly relevant for organizations seeking to establish repeatable and evidence-based AI assurance processes. Potential applications include:

- AI system acceptance and go/no-go decisions
- AI risk and trustworthiness assessment
- Security and robustness evaluation
- Generative AI and agentic AI evaluation
- Procurement and third-party assurance
- Model and system validation
- Continuous evaluation after deployment
- Regulatory, audit, and governance evidence
- High-impact and mission-critical AI assurance

## Relevance to CAIO, CISO, CIO, and AI Governance Functions

The TEVV-Athlon provides a practical bridge between AI governance requirements and measurable technical and operational evidence. It can support:

- **CAIOs** in defining enterprise AI evaluation and assurance strategies
- **CISOs** in integrating adversarial, security, resilience, and misuse testing into AI assurance
- **CIOs and CTOs** in establishing production acceptance and service-quality criteria
- **Risk and compliance leaders** in connecting AI risk registers to measurable controls and evidence
- **Procurement functions** in requiring objective AI evaluation before acquisition or renewal
- **Boards and executive committees** in receiving evidence-based reporting on AI readiness, residual risk, and performance
